How It Works

Enterprise operating discipline, installed at home.

The experience stays simple. Underneath it, we use proven infrastructure tools to keep services private, organized, visible, and easier to recover.

Proxmox

A stable foundation

Proxmox provides the virtualization layer. It separates core workloads from the hardware and gives us controlled maintenance, snapshots, and clear recovery points.

Kubernetes

Health checks and controlled restarts

Kubernetes runs the media services. Health checks catch failed workloads, controllers restart them, and a consistent deployment model makes updates repeatable.

Tailscale

Private access from approved devices

Tailscale creates an encrypted private network between your system and approved phones, tablets, and computers. Services stay off the public internet by default.

Praetor Operations

Managed after installation

Monitoring and alerts help us spot trouble early. We document backups and recovery for the system we install, then maintain it as your needs change.
