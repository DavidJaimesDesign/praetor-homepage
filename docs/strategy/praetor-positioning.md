# Praetor Positioning Notes

## Business Direction

Praetor Home Systems presents private digital-estate infrastructure as a high-trust residential service. The business is aimed at households that care about privacy, continuity, discretion, and local support.

## Audience

- Privacy-conscious households
- Families with meaningful digital archives and sensitive records
- Trusted referral partners such as advisors or household specialists

## Language Boundaries

Lead with:

- Private family infrastructure
- Privacy
- Continuity
- Control
- Trusted local support

Avoid leading with:

- Servers
- Homelab
- Self-hosting
- Commodity IT support

## Design Direction

The site should feel calm, premium, and residential rather than trendy or aggressively technical.
